# agent-skills
1. Timecard Assistant
Gets weekly timecards done in minutes. It pulls each person's scheduled assignments and hours, drafts the timecard, then saves and submits it for approval. It also flags missing, incomplete, and awaiting-approval timecards so nothing slips. Hours come from the schedule rather than guesswork, and nothing is saved or submitted without the user's go-ahead.

2. Project Status Update
Turns "how's this project going?" into a one-minute, ready-to-share status report. It checks milestones, timecards, tasks, hours variance, and logged risks, then summarizes the project's health with named people, dates, and concrete next steps. Project managers no longer have to pull data from five different places.

3. Month-End Close
Gets finance teams from an open, messy period to a clear go/no-go on closing. It finds every unposted journal, invoice, credit note, and cash entry, sorts them into ready, stale, empty, or future-dated, and posts, discards, or reverses them only after confirmation. It then checks the trial balance. It reduces close-day errors and shows exactly what is still blocking the close.

4. Onboarding Kickoff
Turns a closed-won deal into a ready-to-run customer onboarding in a single guided session. It creates the success plan, objectives, KPIs, and playbook, allocates resources, and assigns tasks. Customers start onboarding faster and more consistently, with no duplicate plans and no manual setup.

5. Portfolio Risk Review
Shows which projects need attention across an entire portfolio. It scans every project's milestones and tasks, ranks projects as At Risk, Watch, or On Track, and explains why. Leaders can spot trouble early and focus on the right projects instead of reviewing each one by hand.

These skills bring Certinia PSA, Accounting, and Customer Success work into Claude. They turn multi-step, multi-screen processes into guided conversations that save time and reduce errors, and they never change records without the user's confirmation.
